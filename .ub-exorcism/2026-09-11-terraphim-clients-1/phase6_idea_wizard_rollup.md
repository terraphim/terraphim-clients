## Phase 10 fresh-eyes note (2026-09-12)

Three further cautions for anyone mining this table. (1) The header already warns that the EXP/UF column is extracted from the first 400 characters of each verdict and may name a covering reference rather than a promotion; treat it as a pointer, not as provenance. (2) Round 1's I-03 verdict text states the UF-039 mechanism as `from_utf8_unchecked` at fff-search grep.rs:1710. Lead addendum 4 later established that this line is reached only by `GrepMode::Fuzzy`, which no workspace call site uses, and that the workspace mechanism is `from_utf8_lossy(..).into_owned()` over the live mapping with the `Mmap::map` aliasing contract as the root cause. The round reports are kept as written; do not quote I-03's mechanism forward. (3) I-03 and I-08 are the only NEW_EXP_PROMOTED rows in round 1 and neither names its EXP in the table column, although both name it in the report body.
