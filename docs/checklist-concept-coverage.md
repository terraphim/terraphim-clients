# Checklist terms against knowledge-graph concepts

Generated by `scripts/checklist-journey.py --inventory` from `/home/alex/.config/terraphim/kg` (45 concept articles). Do not edit by hand.

A category is satisfied only when a *concept* credits one of its terms. A term with no backing concept makes its category unsatisfiable, whoever writes the text.

Two different columns, deliberately: **literal match** is a static search of concept names and `synonyms::` lines, while **category reachable** is measured by the CLI itself. Where they disagree the measurement is authoritative - the matcher resolves more than literal substrings, and guessing at that would be worse than saying so.
