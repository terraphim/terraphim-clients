# Encryption

Encryption protects data at rest or in transit by making it unreadable without the key. TLS
covers transit; at rest it usually means an encrypted volume or an encrypted column. The review
questions are about the key and the boundary: where the key lives, who can read it, how it
rotates, and whether anything that should be encrypted sits in plaintext beside it.

synonyms:: encryption, encrypted, ssl, tls, cipher, certificate, key rotation, at rest, in transit
